code licensed under AGPL-3.0-only.

## Uses that do not need permission

You may:

- truthfully refer to the official project, for example “runs on DBMason”;
- link to the official repository;
- state that a project is “compatible with DBMason” when that statement is accurate;
- describe modified software as a “fork of DBMason”, provided your own product
  name is more prominent and you do not imply endorsement; and
- redistribute an unmodified official build with the existing notices intact.

## Uses that need written permission

You must obtain written permission before using the DBMason name or logo:

- as the primary name, domain, package, container image, or logo of a modified
  distribution;
- in a way that suggests sponsorship, approval, or official status;
- on merchandise or paid services as a brand rather than a truthful reference;
  or
- in a confusingly similar product identity.

Modified distributions should choose their own name and visual identity. This
policy does not restrict the rights granted by AGPL-3.0-only to use, study,
modify, or share the software itself, and it does not restrict lawful
nominative or descriptive use.

To request permission, open a private discussion with the maintainer through
the contact options on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/bestmaa).

This policy is a practical project policy, not a statement that any mark is
registered in every jurisdiction and not legal advice.
