# Code of conduct

DBMason welcomes constructive participation from people of every background
and experience level.

Contributors must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people,
avoid harassment and discrimination, protect private information, and respect
security embargoes. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the
community or project.

If a conduct issue cannot be handled publicly, contact the maintainer through
the private contact options on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/bestmaa).
Reports will be reviewed in good faith with attention to the safety and privacy
of everyone involved.
